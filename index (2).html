Our Story

A Gem in the Heart of Gwalior

The Emeraalld was born from a single vision — to create a dining space where flavours are celebrated as art, and every guest feels like royalty. Named after the precious stone, we hold ourselves to the standard of something rare and enduring.

From sizzling grills to delicate desserts, our kitchen is a stage for culinary theatre — where traditional recipes meet contemporary technique, and every plate tells a story.
